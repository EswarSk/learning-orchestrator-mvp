## Program Overview

### What does the speedrun program cover?

The speedrun program is a fast‐paced, 12-week startup program that guides founders through every critical stage of their growth. It kicks off with an orientation to introduce the cohort, then dives into rapid product development—helping founders think through MVP while addressing key topics like customer acquisition and design partnerships.

Throughout the program, startups benefit from expert-led sessions and interactive office hours that cover:

- **Brand Building & Go-to-Market Strategy:** Crafting your story, marketing, and driving product-led growth.
- **Customer Acquisition & Launch:** Securing early users and executing effective launch plans.
- **Fundraising & Strategic Partnerships:** Pitching, navigating investment, and building lasting relationships.
- **Team Building & Operational Scaling:** Developing high-performing teams and refining internal processes for sustained growth.
- **Community & Enterprise Sales:** Building communities, forming strategic partnerships, and landing your first enterprise customers.
- **Product-Market Fit & Demo Day Prep:** Assessing market traction which culminates in a Demo Day to showcase progress.

This comprehensive curriculum is delivered via IRL keynote sessions with luminary speakers and founders, workshops, and office hours—all designed to equip startups with the skills and networks needed to scale quickly.

### What is the program timeline and structure?

The speedrun program is IRL in San Francisco and runs for 12 intensive weeks.

Over the 12 weeks of the program, our team of expert investors and operators guide your startup from idea to market launch. The program moves through sequential modules—each dedicated to key aspects such as rapid product development, go-to-market strategy, fundraising, team building, and operational scaling. Expect regular check-ins, one-on-one office hours, and interactive sessions, culminating in a Demo Day where you present your progress to potential investors.

## Eligibility & Application Process

### Who is eligible for speedrun?

We welcome early-stage founders from all backgrounds. Founders from around the world are welcome to apply, and our [Global Founders Program](https://speedrun.a16z.com/global-founders-program) can help you navigate visas and relocation.

### How do I apply for the speedrun program?

Submit your application [here!](https://speedrun.a16z.com/apply)

### What is the application deadline?

The application deadline for our summer + fall San Francisco cohort (SR007) was May 17, 2026, at 11:59 PM PT.

Missed it? You can still apply anytime — off-cycle submissions are automatically reviewed for the upcoming cohort.

### What if I submit my application after the deadline?

If you miss the deadline, don't stress. We'll automatically review your application for the next cohort.

### Can I apply if I'm a solo founder?

Absolutely. You can apply to speedrun as a solo founder! We value founders who demonstrate the ability to build from scratch and overcome challenges independently. That said, due to the complexities of scaling a startup, we generally prefer teams with multiple founders who bring complementary skill sets. If you’re a solo founder with a strong track record of execution, we strongly encourage you to apply.

### If I have already secured funding, can I still apply to speedrun?

Yes, we aim to be the first check into your company but also accept early-stage companies that have already raised capital.

### Can I reapply if my application is not accepted?

Yes! Although we're not able to re-review teams within a single application period, we understand that early-stage startups are in constant evolution. If you’re not selected for the current cohort, we encourage you to keep refining your product and reapply for a future cohort. Many repeat applicants have been admitted into the program.

### What if my startup is based outside of San Francisco?

That's no problem. We accept founders from around the world, and don't require teams to move their companies. However, because the 12-week in-person component of the program is designed to be immersive, participants must be able to attend on site in San Francisco. If relocation poses a challenge, **or you'll need help navigating visas,** please reach out—we’re open to discussing individual circumstances!

### My startup doesn’t really need the capital. Does it still make sense to apply?

Absolutely. At speedrun, the money is just one element of what we offer. Many of our founders already have access to capital, but they come to speedrun for the hands‑on mentorship, strategic guidance, and network that help accelerate growth. If you’re looking to refine your strategy and build a scalable business quickly, this program is for you.

### I have a great startup idea, but I’m not technical. Will you still fund me?

Our program is designed for teams that can build and iterate on their product internally. This usually means having a technical co-founder or equivalent expertise. If you’re not technical, we recommend partnering with someone who can help you execute your vision effectively.

### Do you provide feedback on application outcomes?

Because we review a high volume of applications, we aren’t able to give detailed feedback on every submission. However, if you’re invited to interview, you’ll receive specific insights about your application and the reasons behind our decision.

### Our team has two ideas. Can we submit separate applications for both?

We suggest focusing on your strongest idea. Concentrating on one concept helps you present a clear, compelling vision that aligns with our program’s focus. Multiple applications can dilute your message, so pick the idea with the greatest potential.

### Can I participate in speedrun while being a full‑time student or working full‑time?

You can apply as a student or an employee, but our 12‑week program requires a full‑time commitment. We expect founders to dedicate themselves fully to building their startup during the program and beyond, to maximize its impact.

## Selection Process

### How does speedrun select teams?

We look for teams that demonstrate:

- A proven capacity to go from zero to one without waiting for permission
- Rapid development and execution
- Unique insights or “earned secrets” that offer a distinct competitive advantage
- Strong, complementary skill sets across technology, business, and go-to-market strategies
- Early signs of market validation (e.g., prototypes, metrics), which are encouraged but not required

We’ve shared a bit more of our thinking about what sets winning applicants apart [in our newsletter](https://speedrun.substack.com/p/what-we-look-for-in-applications).

### How is the review process structured?

Our team reviews applications on a rolling basis, usually within 4–6 weeks. If we’re excited to move forward with your application, we’ll invite you to submit a brief video pitch or schedule a 15-minute interview.

We respect your time, so all interviews will start and end promptly. Following the interview, we may reach out for additional information such as references, data, or further details about your company. After an interview, our goal is to make final decisions quickly and get back to you.

### If speedrun has already invested in a company with a similar idea to mine, does that affect my chances?

We try to avoid direct conflicts, but we also evaluate each team on its own merits. Even if we’ve backed companies in similar spaces, what matters is your unique approach, the strength of your team, and your potential to execute. Overlap in ideas isn’t a barrier.

### What is your conflict policy once you invest in a category via speedrun?

We focus on very early-stage startups in speedrun and prioritize the backgrounds of the founders over the ideas. Thus, the expectation is that the idea will change and evolve, and we may end up investing in multiple companies within the same category, whether in speedrun or A16Z's other funds.

## Investment Terms

### How much does speedrun invest?

We invest up to $1,000,000 in each accepted company. [See more about our deal terms here](https://speedrun.a16z.com/the-deal).

### What are the investment terms?

We have a standard deal for companies accepted to speedrun:

- **Up to $1M in funding**. We invest $500K for 10% upfront in a SAFE and another $500K in your next round within 18 months, so you start your fundraise from a position of strength
- **$10M+ from partners**. Speedrun companies receive cloud, AI, and software credits worth >$10M, as well as priority access to model providers, app stores, and social platforms. Our credits are often enough to fully defray the cost of building an MVP
- **We fund right away**. We accept startups on a rolling basis, wire immediately upon close, and start working together right away
- **Long-term partnership**. We view this as a long-term partnership so we ask for a right to invest in subsequent financing rounds (a “pro rata” right). In many cases, we’ve invested millions of additional dollars to support our companies later on
- **Take care of employees**. We ask companies to set aside an option pool for new employees given our focus on helping founders recruit. We can help set up your pool as well as company incorporation as needed
- **No board seat**. We believe founders should control their boards at this stage

[These standard deal terms are published here](https://speedrun.a16z.com/the-deal).

### Can I seek additional funding while participating?

Yes, the speedrun investment can complement a broader funding round. However, we recommend that founders delay raising additional capital until they've progressed through the program and present on Demo Day—a key part of our process.

## Founder Responsibilities & Success Metrics

### What are founder responsibilities and expectations?

Our program is designed to support your company’s growth every step of the way. To help you make the most of this opportunity, we encourage active participation in both in-person and virtual sessions, interactive workshops, and office hours.

### How do we measure success during the program?

We focus on celebrating your progress through clear, actionable milestones. Throughout the program, you'll work on advancing your product, securing early customers, and refining your fundraising strategy—goals we set together to match your unique vision. By Demo Day, we aim to see tangible progress, such as market traction and a validated business model.

### What if I have a scheduling conflict or need to miss a session?

We understand that startups are dynamic and sometimes unforeseen circumstances arise. If you’re unable to attend a session, please notify your program lead as soon as possible. We offer recorded sessions and one-on-one catch-ups to ensure you stay on track, though we do expect all founders to be in‑person.

### What is the time commitment for speedrun?

Our 12‑week program is designed to be both impactful and manageable. You’ll typically only need to commit to one focused day per week—filled with in‑person sessions, workshops, and office hours—while still having plenty of time to work on your startup throughout the week. This structure ensures you receive high‑quality mentorship and peer learning without overwhelming your schedule.

## Post-Program Support & Alumni Network

### What post-program support and alumni network can I expect?

Even after the program concludes, you’re not on your own. Graduates gain ongoing access to our robust alumni network and exclusive networking events. This long-term support is integral to ensuring that you have the resources needed to scale your company well beyond the program.

## Immigration & Visa Support

### I’m in the US on a work visa. Can I join the program?

In most cases, yes. Our [Global Founders Program](https://speedrun.a16z.com/global-founders-program) has helped many founders navigate visa requirements. If you’re invited to join speedrun, we’ll connect you with the right resources to explore the best options for your situation.

### I don’t currently have a visa. Can I still apply to speedrun?

Absolutely. Immigration hurdles can be a challenge for international founders, but our [Global Founders Program](https://speedrun.a16z.com/global-founders-program) helps admitted founders navigate the high stakes moments like visa petitions, consulate interviews, and border crossing.
