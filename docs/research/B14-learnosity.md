### Engage learners in more ways

Boost learner engagement with content that connects. From media-enabled items and on-screen tools to interactive elements—get more ways to create assessment experiences that resonate.

- #### Wide variety of technology-enhanced items





Earn learner attention with an extensive range of default question types including match & order, graphing & charting, essay, MCQ, and more—or go bespoke by customizing your own.



[Learn more](https://learnosity.com/products/questions/)

- #### Multimedia-ready design





Meet learner expectations with modern assessment authoring tools. Easily embed or upload videos, GIFs, audio files, and more to enrich the learning experience.



[Learn more with our Author Guide](https://authorguide.learnosity.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000439578-File-Upload#:~:text=The%20file%20upload%20question%20type,of%2012%20files%20in%20total.)

- #### Built-in accessibility





From color contrast and screen reader support to closed captions and keyboard navigation—create content that meets learners’ diverse needs.



[Discover more in our documentation](https://help.learnosity.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000754718-Learnosity-s-Approach-to-Accessibility)

- #### Fully featured for greater freedom





Give learners more ways of working with on-screen interactives such as calculators, protractors, rulers, keypads, line readers, and more.



[Learn more with our Author Guide](https://authorguide.learnosity.com/hc/en-us/sections/360000128058-Features-Simple-Features)

- #### Multi-language support





Our pre-built language bundles let you translate the user-interface to overcome language barriers to reach learners and authors on a global scale.



[More in our documentation here](https://help.learnosity.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000758697-Internationalizing-and-Localizing-the-Assessment-Experience)

### Create and collaborate with confidence

Simplify the workflow for authors and reviewers. With centralized communication, at-a-glance status checks, and comprehensive options for customization, Author makes collaboration seamless.

- #### Item bank tagging and search





By embedding Learnosity, your assessment authoring software gains advanced item-tagging and search capabilities that streamline content management.



[Learn more with our Author Guide](https://authorguide.learnosity.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000581278-What-is-Tagging-)

- #### Workflow management





Set your teams up for success by modifying review workflows or customizing them to suit your specific needs. With our remote question authoring software, we make the content review process smoother for large, globally distributed content teams.



[Learn more in our Author Guide](https://authorguide.learnosity.com/hc/en-us/articles/4405304896669-Item-Review-Workflow)

- #### Enterprise-grade data security





Our tightly governed security ensures your proprietary content can only be accessed by the right people.



[Security at Learnosity](https://learnosity.com/platform/security/)

- #### User permissions





Easily create and manage user accounts. Delegate role access, view author activity, and control access to content.



[Discover how with our Author Guide](https://authorguide.learnosity.com/hc/en-us/sections/360000128018-User-Management-Permissions)

- #### World-class, worldwide customer care





Our globally distributed support experts are always on hand to quickly resolve any content management problems that may arise.



[Support at Learnosity](https://learnosity.com/platform/support/)
