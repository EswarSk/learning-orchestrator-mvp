## Insights delivered from agents, visualized for humans

### Build the interface your operation needs

Build interfaces for any use case including customer support, logistics operations, escalation management and more, without waiting on engineering.

### Live agent data, built in

Every app sits directly on Context, with real-time and historical access to any table, view, or variable your agents produce. Apps can trigger workflow actions, log tickets, manage escalations, and surface any data pattern your team needs to act on.

### Build fast, customize as needed

Each app starts from a template with a code editor and live preview built into the platform. For teams that want to move faster, Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) build your first apps as part of deployment so your operation has purpose-built interfaces ready from day one.

## Use intelligence to build your interface

### Describe what you need and AI copilot builds it

Tell the AI copilot what your team needs to see and it generates the app from a template, wires it to your Context data, and sets up the layout which is ready to preview and refine immediately.

### Edit and iterate from chat

Add new views, change data sources, update layouts, and adjust filters directly from the copilot panel with every change landing as a reviewable operation before it goes live.

### Always on, even in the background

Close the panel mid-build and the conversation resumes exactly where it left off when you come back, so complex app builds are never lost or interrupted.
